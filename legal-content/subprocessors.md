## AI training

We confirm that none of our subprocessors uses Customer Data or End User Data to train artificial intelligence models. Specifically:

- **Anthropic** does not train its models on data submitted by API customers under its commercial API terms.
- We do not train our own models on any Customer Data or End User Data.

## Subprocessor changes

If we add a new subprocessor that processes personal information, we will update this page at least 30 days before the change takes effect.

If you object to a new subprocessor on reasonable grounds related to data protection, you may terminate your subscription with effect from the date the new subprocessor begins processing your data, with a pro-rata refund of any prepaid fees for the remainder of the billing cycle.
