Why not a voice AI?

A robot that answers the phone isn't the same as intake.

There are three common ways to handle an enquiry. Only one hands you a lead that's ready to act on.

Voice AI receptionists
Who speaks for you
A synthetic voice, standing in for your business.
What the enquirer gets
A robot phone call — many people can't take one at work, on site, or when they can't speak privately.
What you get back
A call transcript to read through.
Where the data lives
Varies — often offshore.
Web forms & chat widgets
Who speaks for you
Nobody — the enquirer fills in the boxes themselves.
What the enquirer gets
A static form that's easy to abandon halfway.
What you get back
Form fields — unscored and in no particular order.
Where the data lives
Varies.
Bury Digital
Who speaks for you
You do — informed by what we've already gathered.
What the enquirer gets
A normal text conversation — the channel people actually answer.
What you get back
A scored, prioritised lead with a summary and suggested questions for the call back.
Where the data lives
Australia.

It never pretends to be you — it does the legwork, you make the call.
